Engagement venue FAQs

Questions about hosting a roka or engagement in Andheri West.

How many guests can an engagement or roka host at Vira & Reva?

Vira accommodates up to 120 guests for a roka or engagement, while Reva is ideal for intimate ceremonies of 25–40 guests.

Can you arrange the floral stage and décor for the ceremony?

Yes. Floral arches, stage styling, backdrop and aisle décor are coordinated as part of your customised engagement package.

Are vegetarian and Jain menus available for the engagement dinner?

Yes. Vegetarian, Jain and Non-Vegetarian dinner menus are available, crafted by the hotel's Pana restaurant kitchen.

Can I host a small, intimate engagement dinner?

Absolutely. Reva is perfect for an intimate plated engagement dinner for 25–40 guests.

Where is the engagement venue located?

B-43, New Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053 — minutes from Lokhandwala, Versova and Oshiwara.
