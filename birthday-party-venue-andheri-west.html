Birthday venue FAQs

Questions about hosting a birthday in Andheri West.

How many guests can I invite to a birthday party at Vira & Reva?

Vira hosts up to 120 guests in banquet or reception style, while Reva is ideal for intimate birthdays of 25–40 guests. Choose based on your guest list.

Can I host a first birthday or kids' birthday party here?

Yes. First birthdays and kids' birthday parties are a popular choice, with décor, dessert tables and a kids-friendly menu available.

Do you provide the cake and birthday décor?

The banquet team coordinates cake, balloon décor, floral centrepieces and dessert tables as part of your customised package.

Are vegetarian and Jain menus available?

Yes. Vegetarian, Jain, Non-Vegetarian and High-Tea menus are all available, crafted by the hotel's Pana restaurant kitchen.

Where is the birthday venue located?

B-43, New Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053 — minutes from Lokhandwala, Versova and Oshiwara.
