Intimate wedding venue FAQs

Questions about hosting an intimate wedding in Andheri West.

How many guests can an intimate wedding host at Vira & Reva?

Reva is ideal for micro weddings of 25–40 guests, while Vira accommodates up to 120 for a small wedding ceremony and dinner.

Do you provide a wedding planner?

Yes. Marriott Certified Wedding Planners are available to help coordinate your intimate wedding, from timelines to décor and vendors.

Can I host my mehendi and wedding functions here?

Yes. Mehendi, haldi, wedding lunches, ring ceremonies and the wedding itself can all be hosted across Vira & Reva.

Are there rooms for out-of-town wedding guests?

Yes. The hotel has 106 rooms and suites on site, so family and out-of-town guests can stay close to the celebrations.

Where is the intimate wedding venue located?

B-43, New Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053 — minutes from Lokhandwala, Versova and Oshiwara.
