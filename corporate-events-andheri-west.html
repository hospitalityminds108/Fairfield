How many delegates can attend a corporate event at Vira & Reva?

Vira hosts up to 120 delegates in theatre, banquet or reception style, while Reva is ideal for smaller meetings and training sessions of 25–40 attendees.

Yes. Sound systems, microphones, screens and projector setups are available, coordinated by the Marriott banquet team.

Can you arrange tea/coffee breaks and working lunches?

Yes. Tea/coffee breaks, working lunches and full buffet or plated menus are available, crafted by the hotel's Pana restaurant kitchen.

Is the venue suitable for a corporate offsite in Andheri West?

Yes. With 106 rooms on site, flexible meeting space and full catering, the venue is ideal for full-day corporate offsites and team gatherings.
