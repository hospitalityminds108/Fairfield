Privacy Policy

How Vira & Reva Banquets and Fairfield by Marriott Mumbai Andheri West collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy is written for the Vira & Reva Banquets website of Fairfield by Marriott Mumbai Andheri West ("the hotel", "we", "us", "our"). Its structure follows the same broad categories used in the Marriott Group Global Privacy Statement, scoped down to what this particular website actually collects: enquiries submitted through our event planning form, basic site analytics, and newsletter sign-ups. If you go on to book a room, join Marriott Bonvoy, or interact with any other Marriott-branded digital or on-property service, that broader activity is governed by Marriott's own Global Privacy Statement, not by this page.

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy applies to data collected through this website — its pages, its "Plan Your Event" enquiry form, and its newsletter sign-up — and to any related email or phone conversation that follows from an enquiry you submit here. It does not cover Marriott.com, the Marriott Bonvoy app, in-hotel systems, or any third-party site you may be directed to.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: what you choose to give us, and what your browser shares automatically.

Information you provide. When you submit the enquiry form or newsletter sign-up, we collect your name, mobile number, email address, the type of event you're planning, and any additional details you type into the form.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours picks up some technical data as you browse — your general device and browser type, approximate location inferred from your IP address, and which pages you visit — through cookies or similar technologies. This helps the page load correctly and tells us, in aggregate, which sections of the site people actually use.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site uses a small number of cookies, broadly grouped the way most hospitality sites describe them:

  • Essential cookies that let the page and enquiry form function correctly.
  • Preference cookies that remember simple choices you've made on the site during your visit.
  • Analytics cookies that help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors move through the site so we can improve it.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so may make some parts of the site less convenient to use.

4. Why We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your event enquiry, discuss availability, pricing and layouts with you, coordinate planning with our events team ahead of your date, and — only if you've opted in — send occasional offers or updates relevant to Vira & Reva Banquets. We rely on your consent for marketing communications and on our legitimate interest in responding helpfully to enquiries for everything else.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Enquiry details may be shared internally with the hotel's events, catering and front-office teams, and with event-service partners (such as décor, photography or AV vendors) strictly to plan and deliver the event you've enquired about. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the hotel's rights, or in connection with a business transfer.

6. Data Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to your request. If your enquiry turns into a confirmed booking, we retain the related information for the period needed to plan and deliver the event and to meet our record-keeping obligations under applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data appropriately protected.

8. Children's Privacy

This website and its enquiry form are intended for adults planning events, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors through this site.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you've submitted through this site, or to stop sending you marketing communications, at any time using the contact details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law; we'll do our best to honour requests consistent with applicable legal requirements.

10. Other Marriott Services

Fairfield by Marriott Mumbai Andheri West operates under the Marriott Group. If your interaction with us extends beyond this website — for example, booking a stay, joining Marriott Bonvoy, or visiting Marriott.com — that data is handled under the Marriott Group Global Privacy Statement, which covers topics like international data transfers, Marriott Bonvoy preferences, and rights under laws such as the GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws in far more detail than is relevant to this enquiry-only website.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise any of the choices above, please contact the hotel at B-43, New Link Rd, Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400053, or call +91 9833012318.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website or practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date above.
