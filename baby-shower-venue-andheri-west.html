Baby shower venue FAQs

Questions about hosting a baby shower in Andheri West.

How many guests can a baby shower host at Vira & Reva?

Reva is ideal for intimate baby showers of 25–40 guests, while Vira can host up to 120 for a larger gathering.

Can you arrange pastel décor and a dessert table?

Yes. Balloon décor, floral styling, a dessert table and a celebration cake are coordinated as part of your package.

Is a high-tea menu available for a baby shower?

Yes. High-tea, buffet and plated options are available, with vegetarian and Jain-friendly selections.

Can I host a godh bharai (traditional baby shower) here?

Absolutely. The venue warmly hosts traditional godh bharai celebrations with family-style seating and catering.

Where is the baby shower venue located?

B-43, New Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053 — minutes from Lokhandwala, Versova and Oshiwara.
